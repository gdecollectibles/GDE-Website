How a GDE gun is made

From design to signature.

  1. 01

    Design

    Every collection begins as an original GDE design, drawn from history, culture and the figures who shaped them.

  2. 02

    Engrave

    The design is engraved into the slide and frame of a Colt 1911.

  3. 03

    Polish

    The stainless steel is polished by hand.

  4. 04

    Gold plate

    The engraving is hand plated in 24K gold.

  5. 05

    Sign and number

    The RP signature goes under the trigger, and the gun takes its number in a limited edition.

Our family

A family business.

GDE Collectibles (Gun Decor Emporium LLC) is family owned and family run. We design every collection ourselves, and we make each gun to the standard we would want for a piece kept in our own family.

That is why every GDE gun is signed and numbered. These are pieces made to be collected, kept and passed down from one generation to the next.

We put everything into the quality of our guns and the service we give our customers. We encourage you to contact us with any question about a collection, a number or a transfer. When you call or write, you speak directly with the owner.

Every sale follows federal, state and local law. We ship only to licensed FFL dealers, and buyer eligibility and the background check are completed at the dealer before transfer.
