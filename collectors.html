How it works

Sign in. Collect. Save.

  1. 01

    Sign in

    Sign in to your account with Google. Your guns are counted by the email you sign in with, including guns you bought with that email before you had an account.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Each gun counts once its payment has gone through. Three guns get you into the Collectors Club, five into the Legacy Club.

  3. 03

    Share your link

  4. 04

Why we do it

Our customers come first.

GDE is a family business, and we know the people who buy our guns. Many of them come back. They buy a second piece, then a third, and before long they are building a collection.

Those are the people GDE is made for. Every gun we make is signed, numbered and meant to be kept and passed down. A collector who keeps coming back is trusting us with pieces that will stay in their family, and we don't take that lightly.

We would rather thank our serious collectors on every purchase than once.

The details

What counts

Which guns count?

Every gun bought on gdecollectibles.com with your account's email, once it has been paid. Guns from our collections and from the gallery both count, and three guns in one order count as three. Guns you bought from us another way, in person or by phone, count too: contact us and we’ll verify them and add them to your account.

What does the discount apply to?

Everything in the store: every collection and every gallery gun, on every order.

When does it start?

On your next order after the one that brings you to three guns (5%) or five guns (10%), and on every order after that. Legacy Club members get the 10% in place of the 5%; the two don't add up.

How does the friends and family code work?

Every Legacy Club member chooses their own code on their account page. Share it with friends and family: they get 5% off their order when they enter it at checkout. Each code is good for 10 orders; after that, contact us and we’ll set you up with a new one. The code is for people who aren’t in a club themselves: it never adds to a club discount.

Do I need a code for my own discount?

No. Sign in before you check out and your club discount is taken off automatically. Your order summary shows it before you pay.

Do my friends' purchases count?

Yes, when they buy through your link. After your first gun, your account page shows a personal link to share. Every gun a friend buys through it counts toward your club, as well as toward their own. The link doesn’t give your friend a discount; it’s how we know they came from you. It works for 30 days after they click it, for one purchase.

What about refunded or cancelled orders?

They don't count toward your guns.

A question about your status?

Contact us. When you call or write, you speak directly with the owner.
