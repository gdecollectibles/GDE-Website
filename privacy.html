Privacy Policy

Effective June 22, 2026

Information we collect

We collect the contact, buyer, and FFL dealer information you voluntarily submit to respond to inquiries and review purchase requests.

How information is used

Information is used to communicate with you, verify collection availability, review legal eligibility information, coordinate an FFL transfer, and maintain appropriate business records.

Email alerts

If you join our email list, we keep your email address and first name to send you news about new collections, limited releases and offers. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also stop emails from your account page. We do not sell or share your email address.

Account sign-in

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us so we can show the orders placed with that email. We do not receive your Google password. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

Sensitive information

This website does not request Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, payment-card information, or sensitive identity documents.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers, selected FFL dealers, or authorities when reasonably necessary to process a request or comply with law.

Privacy questions may be directed to gdecollectibles@gmail.com or 1-786-931-3566.
