Refund Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

No returns for change of mind

We do not accept firearm returns for buyer’s remorse, manufacturer defects, or other purchase-related reasons. Manufacturer defects are handled through the manufacturer’s warranty where one applies.

Wrong or damaged firearms

We accept a return only when the firearm does not match its listing description or arrives damaged. In that case we will send a replacement if one is available and pay the return and reshipping costs. If no replacement is available, we will issue a full refund to the original payment method and pay the return shipping.

Inspect at your FFL dealer

Please inspect your firearm at your FFL dealer before you take possession of it. If it arrives damaged or does not match the listing, do not complete the transfer: ask the dealer to hold it and contact us right away, so we can resolve it quickly. If it was damaged in transit, keep the box and all packing materials for the carrier’s claim.

After transfer

Once a firearm has been transferred to you, returns are no longer accepted. From that point we can only help arrange a warranty repair where one applies.

Legal non-compliance

You are responsible for confirming that the firearm is legal in your state, city, county and locality before you order. Firearms returned because they do not comply with those laws are subject to a 25% restocking fee plus the shipping and insurance cost of the return, deducted from the refund to the original payment method.

Custom pieces

Special-order, personalized, engraved or otherwise customized pieces cannot be canceled or refunded once work or allocation has begun.

Canceled or unavailable orders

If we cancel your order, or the firearm sells on another marketplace before we can ship it and you decline a matching piece, we refund the full amount you paid to the original payment method.

Shipping and insurance

GDE Collectibles pays for shipping and insurance. You are not charged for either.

Contact

Questions before you pay or accept an FFL transfer? Call 1-786-473-9020 (9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern, Monday to Friday), email info@gdecollectibles.com, or use the website contact form.
