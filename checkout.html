Common questions

Purchase & transfer FAQ

Can I complete a firearm purchase instantly online?

No. The checkout form submits a purchase request for review. Sales are finalized only after compliance, buyer eligibility, and FFL transfer requirements are verified.

Do firearms ship directly to customers?

No. Firearms ship only to licensed FFL dealers.

Are background checks required?

Yes. Buyer eligibility, background checks, and all applicable legal requirements must be completed before transfer.

What FFL information do I need?

You should provide your selected FFL dealer's business name, license number if available, contact information, and address.
