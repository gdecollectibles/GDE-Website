The Craft of the Gold Accent Scroll

Scrollwork on fine guns traces back to the acanthus leaf of Greek and Roman ornament. On the Gold Accent Scroll it covers the slide and carries on over the frame and around the trigger guard, running the whole length of the slide from the rear sight to the muzzle.

The slide and frame are stainless steel, polished bright so every cut of the engraving catches the light. The hammer, trigger, safeties, slide stop and screws are plated in gold, and pearl white grips are each set with Colt's rearing pony in gold.

It is built on a full-size Colt Government Model, chambered in .38 Super with nine rounds in the magazine and one in the chamber, a custom engraved 1911 pistol with gold accents.
