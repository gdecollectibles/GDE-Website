The Craft of the Blue Scroll

Scrollwork on fine guns traces back to the acanthus leaf of Greek and Roman ornament. On the Blue Scroll it is cut into the slide from the muzzle to the rear, on both sides of the pistol.

The steel is finished in Royal Blue, a deep, high-polish blue that sets off the gold parts, with the hammer, trigger, safety, slide stop, screws and pins all plated in gold. A polished silver barrel shows through the ejection port, bright against the blued slide, and white pearl grips are each set with Colt's rearing horse in gold.

It is built on a full-size Colt Government Model Series 70 with a five-inch barrel, chambered in .38 Super with nine rounds in the magazine and one in the chamber, a custom engraved 1911 pistol for the collector who wants classic scrollwork in blue and gold.
