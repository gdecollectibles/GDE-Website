The Meaning Behind the Gold Skull

Memento mori is Latin for “remember that you will die,” and for centuries the skull has carried that message in art. In the 1600s Dutch painters set skulls among flowers and candles in still lifes called vanitas, and in the 1700s pirates flew the skull and crossbones to frighten ships into surrender.

The leaf scrolls around the skulls come from the acanthus of Greek and Roman ornament, which inspired the scrollwork engraved on fine guns. The pistol beneath them has its own history: Colt began selling the 1911 to civilians as the Government Model in 1912, and in Vietnam “tunnel rats” went into enemy tunnels carrying an M1911A1 and a flashlight.
